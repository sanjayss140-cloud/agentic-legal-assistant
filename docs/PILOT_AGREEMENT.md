# PILOT EVALUATION AGREEMENT

**BETWEEN:**
1. **The Provider**: Agentic Legal Assistant Technologies ("Provider")
2. **The Participant**: The Law Firm or Advocate accepting this Agreement ("Pilot Partner")

---

### 1. Purpose of the Pilot
The Provider has developed an artificial intelligence decision-support platform known as **Agentic Legal Assistant** (the "Platform"). The Pilot Partner agrees to test and evaluate the Platform in a private pilot environment solely to assess its workflow utility and verifiable drafting capabilities.

---

### 2. Decision Support — Not Legal Advice
2.1. The Platform provides **automated decision support and legal research verification tools only**. It does **NOT** provide legal advice, legal opinions, or professional legal counsel.  
2.2. The Pilot Partner acknowledges that the Advocate or Legal Practitioner retains exclusive professional responsibility, duty of care, and ultimate supervisory discretion over all pleadings, petitions, filings, citations, and submissions made to any court, tribunal, or client.  
2.3. All outputs generated by the Platform must be independently read, checked, and verified by a licensed advocate prior to reliance or court filing.

---

### 3. Data Confidentiality & Protection
3.1. **Confidential Information**: All case files, petitions, court orders, client references, notes, and correspondence uploaded by the Pilot Partner shall constitute strictly Confidential Information.  
3.2. **Protection Standard**: The Provider agrees to implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards, including application-level AES-256 encryption at rest and strict multi-tenant database isolation.  
3.3. **No Training on Partner Data**: The Provider warrants that neither client documents nor generated drafts will be used to train, fine-tune, or improve public machine learning models.

---

### 4. Intellectual Property Rights
4.1. **Platform IP**: The Provider retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Platform, including software code, architecture, algorithms, and documentation.  
4.2. **Partner IP & Work Product**: The Pilot Partner retains exclusive ownership and copyright of all documents uploaded and all legal petitions, briefs, and drafts produced during the pilot evaluation.

---

5.2. The Provider expressly disclaims all warranties of any kind, whether express, statutory, or implied, including merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, and non-infringement.  
5.3. While the Platform utilizes deterministic citation matching (Gate A), entailment evaluation (Gate B), and corpus registry audits (Gate C), the Provider does not guarantee that court documents or third-party authorities are exhaustively free of typographical or judicial nuance.

---

### 6. Limitation of Liability
6.1. In no event shall either party be liable for any indirect, incidental, punitive, or consequential damages, loss of profits, loss of goodwill, or adverse legal outcomes arising out of the use of or inability to use the Platform.  
6.2. The total aggregate liability of the Provider arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be limited to the total amount paid by the Pilot Partner under this Agreement, or INR 10,000 (Ten Thousand Indian Rupees), whichever is greater.

---

### 7. Term & Termination
7.1. This Pilot Agreement shall remain in effect for the agreed pilot duration (default: 30 days) unless terminated earlier by either party upon seven (7) days written notice.  
7.2. Upon termination, the Pilot Partner may export all matter records, and the Provider shall permanently delete all partner documents, chunks, and embeddings in accordance with the Data Destruction Procedure.
